mini-sdf-csg / blended CSG on signed distance fields

A CSG tree where every operation carries a blend radius k (0 = hard boolean) and a material color, evaluated as an SDF and meshed to watertight triangles with 120 lines of dependency-free JS. Everything on this page is generated live in your browser — no build step, no assets.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Cut faces inherit the cutter's color; blended unions mix materials. Meshes are watertight (every edge shared by exactly two faces). three.js is loaded from a CDN; the geometry kernel itself is this repo's sdfcsg.js.
